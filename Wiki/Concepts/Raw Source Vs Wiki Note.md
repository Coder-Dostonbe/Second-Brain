# Raw Source Vs Wiki Note

## Definition

A **Raw source** preserves captured material as-is (an article, transcript, or note), while a **Wiki note** turns the useful claims from one or more Raw sources into a short, linked, reusable note.

## Why it matters

Keeping the two separate makes every claim in the Wiki traceable back to where it came from, and lets an agent search a small compiled layer instead of re-reading everything every time.
